# Balance evidence

Six stages introduce two resources and four processes each. Inputs form an
acyclic resource graph; consumption stops at zero/reserves and competes in a
stable process order. Lifetime production counts gross output, independent
of downstream consumption. Geometric costs support exact finite bulk sums,
Max and next milestone purchases.

Natural reference strategy (one decision each simulated minute): prioritize
missing processes, pause consumers of the required cost currency, buy a
quarter of affordable Max upstream, buy affordable upgrades/research; perform
first Stellar and Galactic reset, then repeat Universal Genesis. No currency
was injected. This is a simulator using the same Core and content as Unity.

| First reset | Measured game time |
|---|---:|
| Stellar Rebirth | 65 minutes |
| Galactic Collapse | 12.467 hours |
| Universal Genesis | 3.865 days |

The 30-day run reached 24 generators, 60 upgrades, 30 research, 48 achievements
and 12 stories. Follow-up challenge/expedition/talent testing starts from that
late-game state and required 11.207 more simulated days; do not add this result
to a claim that every feature was human-tested in one month.

Evidence: Logs/balance-result.json, progression.csv, content-reachability.json,
extended-content-result.json. Thresholds, costs and rewards are in authored
Resources/content.json; generate-content.mjs is the source authoring tool.
No progression requires purchases. Paid boosts are omitted from challenges.
Human playtesting and tuning for different decision policies remain necessary.
