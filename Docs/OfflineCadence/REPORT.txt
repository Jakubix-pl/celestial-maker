Celestial Maker 2.6.0 - Offline cadence
Final verified Windows release.

IMPLEMENTED
- Live and offline production use the same 1/60-second integration. Every quantum
  advances stock, gross lifetime production, construction and parallel economies.
  Rates are cached only while there is no decision/depletion/unlock boundary;
  elapsed production is never replaced by rate multiplied by the whole absence.
- Purchases, research, previously enabled resets, expeditions and transfers run
  at their simulated decision times. First-time resets remain manual.
- Catch-up yields after a soft 6 ms work budget (checked every 32 ticks) and shows
  progress. Remaining time and report travel together in the save. Newly elapsed
  real time and live-clock backlog are added exactly once. No absence cap.
- A pre-offline restore point protects the original state. Resets inside a batch
  do not write inconsistent intermediate cursors. Autosaves occur between batches.
- Optional Automation > Development Plan selects Energy, Matter or Time after
  first Prestige. The plan controls routing, purchases, focus, laws and unlocked
  universes and repeats previously discovered resets. Existing saves start OFF.
  Stop plan and automatic resets returns these decisions to the player.
- Guided routing buys basic research after Prestige when no explicit queue is
  pending. Manual reserves and construction funding remain protected. Advanced
  research queues retain their Temporal workshop gate.
- Construction base work is 6.6h (previously 6h), growth remains 2.06. This offsets
  the recovery automation so full strategies still finish near 400h. Existing
  construction work is retained in saves.
- Resource-spend reports exclude the numerical underflow clamped at depletion.
  Expedition comparisons use a consistent event-time tolerance.

MEASURED CAMPAIGNS - actual offline tick replay
Visits at 0, 6, 12, ... hours, including the opening. No accelerated first session.
Each absence is fully replayed and serialized/reloaded. The script buys affordable
research and relevant development at visits, chooses the named plan after first
Prestige, and confirms the first reset of each layer. No manual reserves, no paid
boosts. Completion means all Ascension/Genesis nodes, three level-six megastructures
and three universes. Completion can happen before the next visit reveals it.

Time: completion 411.452733h; observed on visit at 414h; first Prestige/Ascension/Genesis 6/12/66h; 70 reloads; 452.8s host wall time.

All three meet the predefined 360-440h range. These are scripted strategies,
not a guarantee for every player's choices or protected stock. Repeated endgame

The nine event-accelerated active/hourly/check-in runs also meet the range;
full tick campaigns, not the accelerator, substantiate the six-hour result.

TICK AUDIT
Three complete six-hour fixtures: early, repeat-reset and parallel economies.
Each replays 1,296,000 full ticks. Compared against live FixedStepClock and a
serialized interrupted replay. Resource/lifetime balances, purchases, resets,
parallel balances and construction match to 1e-7 relative numeric tolerance.
Reports balance against the scale of their ledger terms (important after large
reset losses); exact bit identity is not promised for floating-point arithmetic.
The reset fixture executed 288 Prestige, 36 Ascension and 1 Genesis resets;
the parallel fixture claimed 249 expeditions and executed six transfers.

The retired event accelerator is a separate diagnostic, not the authority for
60 Hz balances. It matches the early/reset fixtures numerically. In the synthetic
parallel fixture initialized with 1e18 stocks, purchases/resets/projects/transfers
match, but some final resource quantities differ. That numerical equivalence test
failed in the first audit and remains explicitly recorded as oracleMatch=false.
The final parallel cross-check asserts progression only; live/offline/resumed tick
balances still require full numeric agreement. Forecasts based on event stepping
remain approximate. Campaign timing above uses full tick replay.

Long absences: fully tested 1h, 6h and 24h. Queues of 7, 30 and 365 days were tested
for bounded processing and lossless remaining-time reload after 10,000 ticks;
a full year was NOT replayed. Large absences can take substantial processing time.
